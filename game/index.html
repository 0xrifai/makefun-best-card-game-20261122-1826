    TIDEBREAK DUEL

    A card duel on a living reef. You are the Tidebreaker; across the table sits the Reef Warden, guarding the last pearl of the drowned city.

    💧Wave · strikes 🔥Ember · fast 🍃Kelp · heals 🐚Coral · guards
    Goal: reduce the Warden's life from 30 to 0 before yours falls.
    Each turn: your tide grows by 1 (up to 8), you draw a card, then play cards by clicking them or pressing 1–7.
    Resonance: two cards of the same suit in one turn — the second gains +2.
    Read the Warden: its intent is shown above its hand. When it gathers tide, a heavy card is coming.

    Keyboard: 1–7 play a card · Space ends turn · N new duel

    🌊 VICTORY! 🌊

    The Warden bows and the reef opens. The last pearl is yours — the tide breaks in your favour.

    THE TIDE TAKES YOU

    Your life ebbs to zero and the Warden keeps the pearl — this time. The reef remembers every duel. Come back stronger.
